## Problem
The Stage 57 whole-bundle governance report correctly denies approval, but did not expose whether an organization actually has two separately eligible *procedural reviewers* for the clinical and financial scopes, or whether the source author is the sole potential reviewer. This operational staffing analysis can be implemented without assuming an approved signatory policy, creating new signing permissions, or granting anyone publication authority.

## Delivered
- `src/lib/catalog-signatory-planning.ts`: pure fail-closed reviewer-capacity calculator. Separates the **potential clinical** (`catalog.manage` and `catalog.reference_ranges.manage`) and **potential finance** (`catalog.manage` and `pricing.manage`) reviewer pools. Counts dual-domain management permissions but **does not confuse one dual-domain user with two independent people**; reports availability of at least one distinct clinical/financial pair. Excludes the source author, inactive users, users without currently active branch grants, users with revoked/irrelevant restricted departments, and those lacking active management grants. Reports potential reviewers with MFA configured; MFA is not itself an electronic signing credential.
- Stage 57 existing **author-only** `GET /api/catalog/imports/[id]/bundle-drift?governance=1` now appends `signatoryPlanning` to the counts-only report, using a **live tenant-locked RepeatableRead** query of current organization's active users, active role statuses/permissions, branch grants and department scopes. Hard limit of 1,000 users; excess fails closed. No names, emails, usernames, identifiers, passwords, MFA secrets, clinical values, price amounts or raw JSON leave the endpoint. Audit records only category counts and policy-gap codes.
- The existing bilingual author UI displays current *potential reviewer capacity*, cross-domain management overlap, distinct pair availability, and number of potential MFA-enabled reviewers; explicitly warns this **is not a signatory designation**.
- `FORMAL_SIGNATORY_ROLES_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, `SIGNATURE_AUTHENTICATION_POLICY_UNDEFINED`, and `SIGNATURE_EVIDENCE_AND_VERSIONED_APPLICATION_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` always remain blocking. Optional staffing gaps: no separate possible clinical reviewer, no separate finance reviewer, or inability to form a pair. All responses contain hard `qualificationToSign:false`, `signaturePolicyApproved:false`, `signingEnabled:false`, `clinicalApprovalAllowed:false`, `financialApprovalAllowed:false`, `liveWrites:false`. Existing Stage 57 final approval blockers are unchanged.
- **No database migrations; no new role/permission names granted, no signatory assignment and no medical/financial changes.**

## Remaining policy decisions and guardrails
This is **staffing-capacity planning, not electronic signature design**. The organization must still decide and approve the formal clinical director/medical signatory, financial price authorizer, branch coverage and substitute arrangements, authentication and re-authentication requirements, signed evidence/attestation/key rotation, maker-checker independence for real activation, immutable historical changes and effective dates, with legal/clinical validation before go-live. Existing `catalog.reference_ranges.manage` / `pricing.manage` are **not final approval entitlements**.
Full clinical/financial/hardware/analyzer/barcode end-to-end acceptance, disaster-recovery drills and regulatory sign-off remain deferred—not passed. Public-repo visibility unchanged; separate analyzer bridge PR #128 is untouched. All master scope remains.
