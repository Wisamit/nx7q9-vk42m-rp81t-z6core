# Stage 70 — Per-Draft Procedural Review Decision Isolation

## Problem
Stage 65–69's independent policy review discovery fetched up to 201 AuditEvent decision records for up to 100 candidate draft events. A single draft with a third malformed/duplicate decision, or any page with over 200 decisions in aggregate, returned `policy_queue_decision_limit_exceeded` (409) and hid every otherwise valid independent review opportunity in the page. A global page limit is not the right unit of clinical/financial independence.

## Change
- `src/lib/catalog-policy-queue-decision-plan.ts`: pure fail-closed per-draft decision count plan. `POLICY_QUEUE_REVIEWS_PER_DRAFT = 2` (CLINICAL, FINANCIAL); reject malformed, repeated, foreign or fractional grouped counts; identify drafts with 3+ AuditEvent records and **exclude only those drafts**. Return a 2×safe-draft bounded decision fetch with a sentinel.
- `src/app/api/catalog/imports/policy-review-queue/route.ts`: read grouped AuditEvent counts by `entityId` for the candidate draft IDs across the full organization (all branches), preserving Stage 69 wrong-branch poisoning visibility. Fetch only records for candidate IDs with 0–2 decisions. Verify exact per-draft observed count, branch, author separation, review digest/structure, unique domain, unique reviewer; suppress any failed draft without exposing decision payloads. Refuse a malformed or inconsistent snapshot rather than trusting partial decision data. **No global 201 decision cap**; large volumes across independent batches are supported.
- The rest of the existing scope remains: a 100-AuditEvent keyset window, 30 eligible entries/page, Stage 67 20-minute HMAC reviewer/tenant/branch cursor and paired audit watermark, full 1,000-revision history, all staged identities and six live-master checks, original+live permissions, branch/department intersections, 30-second organization-locked RepeatableRead transaction.
- Update Stage 64 and Stage 65 test expectations to guard the new policy. Add `tests/stage-70-policy-queue-per-draft-decisions.test.ts` for >200 legitimate aggregate decisions, independent corruption, count validation, structure and authorization guarantees.
- Add MariaDB integration test generating 200 valid decisions across 100 independent draft IDs, an available unreviewed candidate, 3-decision poison, cross-branch and author-review poison, duplicate-domain poison, plus an outside-tenant event to confirm tenant isolation. The CI pipeline runs this alongside all prior clinical, billing and import safety tests.

## Safety and rollout
- No clinical or financial signatures: procedural comments remain `PROCEDURAL_REVIEW_ONLY` and cannot activate or apply catalog prices, reference intervals, units, lab tests, or reports. `approval=false`, `signingEnabled=false`, `activation=false`, `liveWrites=false`.
- **No database migration** and no environment/key rotation required. Event history remains append-only; a draft with 3+ review events is hidden and remains subject to audit remediation rather than silently cleaned or reinterpreted.
- These changes affect only independent draft review queue discovery; independent reviewer GET and POST still recheck all permissions/history/live masters from scratch. Scope constraints and final clinical/financial governance decisions remain deferred until explicit approval by the user.
- Stage 10 Analyzer Bridge PR #128 is unrelated and remains draft. Deploy only after CI succeeds on the exact feature commit and again on the independently merged main commit; manual UAT remains deferred until system completeness as agreed.
