**Confirmed baseline:** Stage 53 merged `main` SHA `344539b7d9c54a28720f71684a7afb72655165f6`, independent CI #973 SUCCESS, Hostinger deployment explicitly confirmed by owner before work. Repository PUBLIC. All 88 sections of `02_MASTER_SCOPE.md` remain the binding target scope.

## Why this stage is deliberately NOT clinical/financial activation
Electronic signature and final signatory policy are identified as open production decisions in `09_DECISIONS_AND_GUARDRAILS.md` and the master scope's electronic approval section. A single operator with `catalog.manage` should not be allowed to self-approve imported reference intervals, test configurations, or prices. Stage 53 created author-scoped triage evidence; Stage 54 introduces a separate **second-person procedural finding disposition** as a necessary step toward governable decisions. `CONFIRM_FINDING` means an expert confirms that the discrepancy merits attention; **it does not approve the imported value or its release**.

## Implemented
- New `src/lib/catalog-expert-disposition.ts`: validates two-person separation (batch author/flagger vs reviewer), category and *current plus original-session* domain grants, Stage 53 HMAC source/live observation freshness, latest triage flag and open Draft batch. Clinical domain covers TEST/COMPONENT/UNIT/REFERENCE_INTERVAL and requires `catalog.reference_ranges.manage`; financial covers PRICE_LIST/PRICE_LIST_ITEM and requires `pricing.manage`. All require `catalog.manage`.
- `PUT /api/catalog/imports/[id]/bundle-drift`: strictly bounded 1 KiB JSON with `flagEventId` and `disposition` only. Reuses full tenant/org row lock, RepeatableRead six-class source and live scopes, 5,000-row validation and current branch/department permissions. **Never trust the browser for the source/live observation token**; compare fresh HMAC of exact source/live JSON to Stage 53's stored audit fingerprint. Require latest flag and different actual reviewer. A stale signal is rejected; a previously decided flag cannot receive a second conflicting decision.
- Decisions are **immutable audit events** with action `CATALOG_BUNDLE_EXPERT_DECISION_RECORDED`, `entityType: CatalogExpertReviewFlag`, `entityId` equal to original flag's AuditEvent ID. Stores actor, branch, category, review-only disposition `CONFIRM_FINDING` or `RETURN_FOR_CORRECTION`, fingerprint, source verification metadata, and explicit `approved:false, activated:false`. Clinical, financial and source master records are never updated.
- A separate `GET /api/catalog/imports/expert-review-queue` returns bounded, branch/department/tenant-filtered open referral **metadata only** to independently authorized specialists. No source or live values are returned. Current persisted role grants are re-evaluated. Previously decided or latest-referral-superseded records are suppressed. A second-person queue's active branch must match the referral branch.
- New bilingual `/catalog/expert-reviews` page, linked from the Test Catalog; separate confirm finding / return for correction actions. No clinical or financial approval/activation buttons.
